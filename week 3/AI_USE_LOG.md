## Purpose of AI use
I used ChatGPT as a coach, critic and classmate to help me interpret my descriptive statistics, question my conclusions and improve my understanding of the mean and median.

## How I used AI
- Asked AI to interpret the mean, median and percentage changes in my results.
- Used AI to help identify which interpretations were supported by the data and which required further evidence.
- Used AI to help structure an audit table containing claims, colours, decisions and explanations of how each claim was checked.
- Used AI to simulate a confused classmate who believed the mean was always the best measure of typical price.
- Asked AI to help identify where my first draft might have made claims beyond what the data allowed.
- Asked AI for help formatting my work in Markdown using VS Code.

## What I kept from AI suggestions
- Explanations of how extreme observations can affect the mean more than the median.
- Suggestions for distinguishing observations, possible explanations and causal claims.
- Ideas for questioning whether the mean or median better represents typical prices.

## How I checked AI's suggestions
I compared numerical claims with my own calculations from the cleaned and flagged datasets. I reviewed interpretations critically and did not treat possible explanations as proven facts. I also checked the percentage changes against my calculated results.

## Limitations
AI can make unsupported interpretations or numerical mistakes. I therefore treated its responses as suggestions rather than verified results and checked the claims against my data.
